# RX — EXP-002

Research Experience records the researcher's changing state while conducting EXP-002.

## Loop
`Research State → Trigger → Experience → Interpretation → State Update → Next RQ`

## Record
Capture:
- what became noticeable
- what was expected
- what was surprising
- confidence change
- method/view choice
- interpretation change
- unresolved uncertainty
- next question

## Boundary
RX records experience and interpretation. It is not Evidence.
Evidence requires an observed/result artifact with provenance and evaluation.

## Example
```yaml
experiment: EXP-002
trigger: "first temporal walkthrough"
experience: "movement made the relation between entrance and central void noticeable"
interpretation: "the relation may be temporal rather than purely geometric"
confidence_before: 0.40
confidence_after: 0.65
state_update: "temporal relation is now a candidate research variable"
next_question: "Can observers reproduce this relation without animation?"
```
