# EXP-002 Counterexamples

Counterexamples are first-class research outputs.

## C-001 — Static equivalence
Animation reveals a relation that is already derivable from plan, section, elevation, or static 3D.

**Effect:** weakens H1.

## C-002 — Attention illusion
The apparent discovery is caused by camera movement, editing, lighting, or motion salience rather than architectural information.

**Effect:** qualifies H1.

## C-003 — Observer disagreement
Observers do not reliably identify the same temporal/spatial relation.

**Effect:** challenges explanatory/reproducibility claims.

## C-004 — Representation artifact
The renderer introduces geometry, timing, or lighting assumptions absent from the source architecture.

**Effect:** invalidates the affected comparison unless controlled.

## C-005 — Animation is merely convenient
The same information can be extracted numerically or from static views with equal or better reliability.

**Effect:** limits the claim to communication/convenience rather than research value.
